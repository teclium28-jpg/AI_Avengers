Accounting, fixed before implementation:
- Net D/R/C/Lt/Lc/T/U are active dispatch/receipt/collection/transport loss/center
  loss/transport return/center return events. Exact authorized reversals cancel one
  original; downstream balances must remain possible.
- Transport balance = D - R - Lt - T; center balance = R - C - Lc - U.
- In-transit is a reported current subset of transport balance, not another flow.
  A subsequent transport-changing event invalidates that observation; unknown is
  null. Unexplained transport = transport balance - (current in-transit report or 0).
- Zero receipt/collection/in-transit reports are allowed and differ from absence.
  Returns do not invent reusable stock or erase gross dispatch commitments.
- Invalid transitions create a separate immutable conflicting report and review;
  they never enter the accepted ledger. Delayed event dates remain source evidence;
  the append order controls operational validation, with both times retained.
